Find your cube’s Bluetooth address on Windows

  1. Open chrome://bluetooth-internals/#devices in Chrome or edge://bluetooth-internals/#devices in Edge.
  2. Select Start Scan and turn a face to wake the cube. Copy the Address next to your cube’s name.
  3. Stop the scan, paste the address into the field above, and select Connect Cube again.
